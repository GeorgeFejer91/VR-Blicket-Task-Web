The game is a stylized procedural model. Its cubic dark body has no raised upper handle; the broad red top, lower-front BLICKET nameplate, sinking platform, cyan scanner, gold/red result flashes and synthesized tune are game adaptations. The current platform is 2.7 scene units wide, with two object centers 1.34 units apart inside the well. That width is an interaction requirement for pair evidence, not a claim about the physical apparatus dimensions. The scanner is neutral for both outcomes and appears only after the complete placed subset arrives. The nameplate glows gold only after activation. All task objects retain identical matte gray materials. Do not claim an exact apparatus or audio reproduction.

Original photographs/video frames are reference material and are not bundled in this repository. The public game uses geometry and synthesized Web Audio effects rather than embedding people, footage or externally hosted images. Keep the source links here for future review.
