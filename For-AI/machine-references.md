The game is a stylized procedural model. Its cubic dark body has no raised upper handle; the square red top, lower-front BLICKET nameplate, sinking platform, cyan scanner with a whole-object light wash and surface bounce, gold/red result flashes and synthesized tune are game adaptations. Do not claim an exact apparatus or audio reproduction. The scanner, wash and bounce are neutral for both outcomes and appear only after the object reaches the well's lip. The nameplate glows gold only after a hidden blicket activates the detector. The blocks retain the existing visible identities and hidden causal data.

Original photographs/video frames are reference material and are not bundled in this repository. The public game uses geometry and synthesized Web Audio effects rather than embedding people, footage or externally hosted images. Keep the source links here for future review.
