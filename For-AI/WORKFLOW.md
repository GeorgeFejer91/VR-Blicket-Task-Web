4. Run applicable gates. Distinguish browser evidence from tests and scientific assumptions. Review the full diff for contradictions and sensitive content.
5. Stage explicit paths, commit validated work, and push when authorized. Never force-push, bypass protection/secret scanning, or stage unrelated files. Verify remote HEAD and deployment.
6. This is the public browser repository; page files live at root. Do not copy the private archive or change visibility on history containing emails. A clean successor needs an explicit migration with reviewed content and history.
7. Handoff: report behavior, evidence, limitations, Git/deployment status, and links. Do not maintain transcripts or duplicate diaries in For-AI.
